Macaroni and Cheese was invented in southern Italy during the 13th century

The dish was orginally in a cookbook called liber de coquina and was called de lasanis, which involved layering square sheets of pasta with grated cheese

Today the dish is known as Mac'n'Cheese and is very common among households since it is inexpensive and easy to make

Ingredients

  • Uncooked elbow macaroni 16oz
  • Evaporated milk 12oz
  • Whole milk 2 1/2 cups
  • Butter 4 tbsp
  • Sharp cheddar cheese (freshly shredded) 3 cups
  • Salt 1 tsp
  • Black pepper 1/2 tsp

Instructions

  1. Lightly grease the inside of a 4-to-6-quart crock pot with nonstick cooking spray or a bit of butter to prevent the edges from scorching.
  2. Add the uncooked macaroni, evaporated milk, whole milk, salt, pepper, and garlic powder directly to the crock pot. Stir well to make sure the seasonings are evenly distributed. Scatter the cubes of butter and cream cheese evenly over the top.
  3. Stir in 2 cups of the shredded cheddar cheese, saving the remaining 1 cup for later. Make sure the macaroni is pressed down into the liquid as much as possible.
  4. Cover with the lid and cook on LOW for 1 hour. Open the lid and give everything a thorough stir to break up any clumps of pasta and distribute the melting cheeses.
  5. Cover and cook on LOW for another 30 to 45 minutes. Taste a noodle—once the pasta is tender, turn the crock pot to WARM. Stir in the remaining 1 cup of cheddar cheese, put the lid back on for 5 minutes until it melts, and serve immediately!
